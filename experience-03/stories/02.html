AI Understands Even My Typos.

Sometimes I make typos when I'm typing.

At first, I corrected them before sending.

But one busy day, I just hit Enter without fixing them.

AI understood me perfectly.

After that, when I was busy, I just left the typos as they were.

No problem.

Sometimes I hit the wrong key and strange characters come out.

Most of the time, it still understands.

Even when the sentence is a mess, it looks at the context and figures out what I'm trying to say.

Smart AI.

But there's one problem.

These days, I don't really care when I make a typo.

It understands anyway.

I'm getting sloppier with spelling, too.

Hang on.

At this rate, AI is going to keep getting smarter,

and I'm going to become a human who can't even spell properly.
