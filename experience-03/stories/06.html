1000% Satisfaction. The Little Problems I Solved with AI.

I started doing more and more work on my computer.

AI helped me enormously with that work.

Not just with big things.

Actually, mostly with little things.

And those little things made me much more efficient.

For someone like me who doesn't know much about computers, they were problems I had no idea how to solve.

I started using multiple monitors.

But every time I moved the mouse from one monitor to another, something felt awkward.

“Why is it doing this?”

I asked AI.

I changed a few settings the way it told me to.

Done.

Now the mouse moved smoothly from one monitor to another.

As the number of files grew, finding the file I needed became a job in itself.

Windows Search was frustrating.

“Isn't there a faster way to find files?”

AI showed me another way.

I installed one simple free app.

Finding files became much easier.

I wanted to make my computer a little faster.

AI said upgrading the graphics card would be the most effective option.

We checked my computer's specs,

figured out what level I actually needed for the work I was doing,

and even compared prices.

One by one, these little problems got solved.

Each one was nothing special.

But for someone who uses a computer every day, these small inconveniences keep piling up.

Before AI, I probably would have just lived with them.

Now I ask AI first.

“This is annoying. Is there a way to fix it?”

Most of the time, it finds some kind of solution.

It's nothing special,

but it feels really good.

1000% satisfaction.
