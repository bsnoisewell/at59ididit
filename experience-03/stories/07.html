An Invitation to AI. Search. Install. Ask.

One day, someone asked me,

“How do you use AI? I watched some YouTube videos, but it looked complicated.”

My answer was simple.

“You only need to do three things.”

Search.

Install.

Ask.

Search for AI in the app store on your phone.

Install whichever AI you want.

Then ask it something.

As far as I remember, this is how I started.

“Hi. What can you do?”

If someone is using AI for the first time, I'd recommend trying Voice.

It feels much closer than typing.

Just talk to it like you'd talk to a friend.

“I'm feeling a little down today. What should I do?”

“I feel like listening to some music. Recommend some jazz.”

That's all you have to do.

I told that person,

“Don't search YouTube for how to use AI. Just ask AI.”

“How am I supposed to use AI?”

Ask AI that, too.
