What a Cruel Question. Who's Better at Coding, Codex or Claude?

This came up in a conversation not long ago.

“Who's better at coding, Codex or Claude?”

Thinking about it,

that's a pretty cruel question.

If they were people,

it would be like asking,

“Who's better?
Your little brother,
or your friend's little brother?”

That's the kind of question it is.
