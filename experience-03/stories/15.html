AT 59, I DID IT WITH AI. EXPERIENCE #03 · BOOK #03

15.
It Told Me to Buy a Template. Then It Made One Itself.

This was when I first started building the site.

I asked AI what I would need.

One of the things it mentioned

was a template for the website.

It told me about free template sites,

paid template sites,

and even how much they cost.

Hmm.

Should I buy one?

But I never actually bought a template.

Because AI drew a main image I hadn't even asked for.

It was pretty good.

I decided to use it as it was.

Then AI put together the entire page.

It arranged the overall layout and the elements I needed

around that image.

Huh?

What about the template?

I didn't need one anymore.

At first, it told me to go find a template.

In the end,

it made one itself.

Couldn't you have done that from the beginning?
