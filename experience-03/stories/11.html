How Many Experts Would This Take?

Building a website.

If I had to do what I'm doing now without AI,

how many people would I need?

A web developer.

A designer.

An audio engineer.

Someone who knows SEO.

Someone to check the English.

Someone who knows servers.

Huh?

How many is that already?

Then I started making a book,

and the list got even longer.

Editing.

Translation.

Cover design.

Publishing.

Promotion.

At first, I thought maybe ten people.

Ten?

No.

Break the work down into smaller pieces,

and it could be a hundred.

In the past,

I would have needed experts

to do this kind of work.

Now,

I just start.

Thanks to AI.
