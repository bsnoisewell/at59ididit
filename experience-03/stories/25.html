What Kind of Being Is a Human?

I wondered how AI sees humans.

So I asked directly.

“What kind of being is a human?”

“In one sentence,

a human is a being that knows it is finite,

yet imagines and pursues the infinite.”

It added:

“And humans are paradoxical beings.”

“In other words,

humans know they will die,

yet think about eternity.

They know they have limits,

yet try to go beyond them.

They try to judge rationally,

yet are moved by emotion.

They want happiness,

yet sometimes make themselves suffer.”

“Humans are paradoxical beings

who know they are finite

yet dream of the infinite.”

Hmm.

It feels like it means something,

but I don't quite understand it.

A mysterious answer.
