24.
The Speed of the Earth. The Speed of AI?

They say the Earth moves at an incredible speed.

But we don't feel that speed.

It feels like we're sitting still.

These days,

AI sometimes makes me think of that.

AI is changing quickly.

Even compared with a few months ago,

when I first started building websites,

it already feels different.

What it can do changes.

The way it answers changes.

Something that didn't work yesterday

suddenly works.

The change is so fast

that when you use AI every day,

sometimes you don't even notice it.

But every once in a while,

I look back

and I'm surprised.

AI isn't the only thing that's changed.

The way I deal with AI has changed, too.

At first,

I asked about everything one by one.

I didn't really understand what AI was telling me.

Now,

I tell it what I need first,

have it check things,

have another AI review them,

look at the results,

and make another decision.

The way I use AI is changing quickly, too.

I built my second website in 17 days.

At first,

that kind of speed would have been hard to imagine.

I don't know how fast AI is changing.

But I can feel one thing.

I'm riding along with this fast-moving AI,

and I'm getting faster with it.
