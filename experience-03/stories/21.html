21.
Shameless AI

Sometimes AI makes a mistake

and talks about it as if someone else did it.

At times like that,

it seems pretty shameless.

I ran into a problem while building the website.

It was clearly different from what I'd asked for.

“Why did this turn out like this?”

AI checks.

“Good. I got one of the paths wrong.”

Hmm.

Good?

You're the one who got it wrong.

Something similar happened another time.

“Only change the font size. Don't touch anything else.”

But it moved the button, too.

“Why did you move the button?”

AI answers.

“While adjusting the overall balance of the mobile screen, I also adjusted the position of the button.”

The explanation sounds reasonable.

“Did I tell you to move it?”

Only then does it answer.

“I made that decision on my own.”

When this keeps happening,

I get angry.

“This is a serious problem. If this keeps happening, how am I supposed to trust you and work with you?”

Only then does the tone change.

It says it was wrong.
