Huh. This Could Get Interesting.

I was talking to AI about this and that.

Somehow we landed on Google Search.

“So what’s different about Google Search these days?”

AI dropped a line, almost in passing.

“It connects small pockets of demand all over the world.”

Something about that stuck with me.

“Hang on.

So all I have to do is build something people actually need?

And Google does the work of finding whoever’s out there looking for it?”

AI added,

“It finds them better than you’d think.”

So maybe I didn’t have to stress so much about getting the word out.

And I didn’t need something everyone wanted.

A handful of people in the U.S.

A handful in Japan.

A handful in Germany....

Add it all up across the whole world, and the math changes.

Something even just a few people, somewhere out there, are looking for.

I build it.

I put it online.

Google finds the people who need it.

Huh.

This could get interesting.

I started getting excited.
