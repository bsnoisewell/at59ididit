11.
Possible? Then Let's Try It.

I wanted the first screen of the website to have a row of different cards.

"Can you make that?"

"Yes. It's possible."

So we did.

Then I thought it would be nice if people could hear a quick preview just by hovering over a card.

"Can you make the sound play for a moment when I hover over a card?"

"Yes. It's possible."

"Isn't that difficult?"

"It's a common feature on the web."

So we did.

The sound played, but with nothing happening on the screen, it felt a little flat.

"Hang on. Can you make a waveform move with the sound?"

"Yes. It's possible."

Possible again.

"This one sounds a little harder."

Something about analyzing audio data, something about the Web Audio API....

More words I didn't know.

"Okay, never mind all that. You're saying you can do it, right?"

"Yes."

"Then let's try it."

A waveform started moving on the screen.

I didn't really know what made it move.

I didn't know what code it took, either.

Come to think of it, this kept happening while I was building the website.

I had an idea.

I said it out loud.

AI said it was possible.

I said, let's try it.

And then, something that had been in my head just moments before was suddenly alive and moving on the screen.
