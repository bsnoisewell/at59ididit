5.
Hang On. What If I Turned This Into a Website?

I'd tried YouTube before.

Back then, I'd learned a little about making video and sound with AI.

Talking about websites reminded me of what I'd been doing back then.

Hang on.

Could I do on a website what I used to do on YouTube?

But the web wasn't YouTube.

First, I had to create and refine noise people actually liked falling asleep to.

Once I had the raw sound, I had to stitch it together seamlessly.

Easy to say. Not easy to do.

It was delicate work.

I dug through Google.

I checked Google Trends.

I spent a good while on market research.

There were similar sites out there, but the competition didn't seem too intense.

Alright. Let's build a black-screen noise website.

A site people could leave on while they slept.

And since the screen would be black, I figured maybe it would be a little easier to build.

Then came the deciding factor.

My inner do-gooder kicked in.

I'd always wanted to make something that actually helped people.

Helping people sleep better.

Perfect.

One last question for AI.

"Could this actually become popular?"

AI said it had potential.

I didn't know yet that AI had a habit of giving positive answers.
