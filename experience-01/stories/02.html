2.
I Know Nothing. Is That Okay?

I knew building a website was supposed to be hard.
And there seemed to be a lot to do.

Buy a domain.
Create the images.
Write the code.
Set up a server.
Make it work on both PC and mobile.
Connect everything to the server.
...

Doing this alone? I couldn't even picture it.

I'd never studied design.
I knew even less about coding.
I didn't even know where a server was supposed to be.

"I know nothing about this.
And I'm on my own.
Can I still do it?"

AI answered.
"Yes. You can do it on your own."

I stared at the screen for a moment.
"Wait... I can?"

I wasn't convinced.
"Hang on.

I thought there were a ton of things to do.
Images, coding, connecting the server, buying a domain...

You're saying you can help me with all of that?"

"Yes."

"Then what's left for me to do?"
"You just decide what kind of website you want, Captain."

Still skeptical.

And that's how I started building my first website.
