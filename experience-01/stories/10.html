10.
Hang On. Can You Do This?

After working side by side with AI for a while, I realized something.

AI doesn't usually tell you what it can do unless you ask.

When you're focused on solving whatever problem is right in front of you, it's easy to miss things AI already knows how to do.

I was working on the first screen of the website.

I was trying to figure out the right background color and checking the aspect ratio.

I tried one font after another.

Then it occurred to me.

Maybe AI could do this.

I simply took a screenshot.

And showed it to AI.

"This is what I've been working on.

Why don't you take a shot at it?”

Wow.

It came back with something pretty impressive.

"I don't think the background color and text color work very well together."

"Any other suggestions?"

It even gave me specific color codes that worked.

Of course, AI isn't good at everything.

But a lot of the things I was struggling with were things AI could already do.

The problem was, I kept forgetting that.

So every now and then, I stop in the middle of what I'm doing.

And ask.

"Hang on. Can you do this?"
