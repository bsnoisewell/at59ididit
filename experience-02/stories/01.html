Taking On My Second Website. BabySweetDreams.com

I built my first website.

I thought building just one was already a pretty big deal.

I was going to take a little break once it was done.

But on the very day I launched my first website, I was already thinking about a second one.

Strange.

Maybe seeing something I'd imagined actually come to life gave me energy.

While building the first site, I'd also learned a few things about sleep.

Things like how long it usually takes people to fall asleep,

and how noise can sometimes help with sleep.

Then I became interested in babies and sleep.

What if I combined lullabies and noise to help babies fall asleep comfortably?

Sounds they could listen to as they drifted off.

Pictures they could look at peacefully.

A site they could watch for a little while before falling asleep, with the screen gradually fading to black.

Hmm.

This could be interesting.

It seemed harder than the first website.

Sound alone wouldn't be enough.

I'd need pictures.

I'd need lullabies.

I'd need movement.

And because babies would be watching it, I'd have to be even more careful.

I asked AI.

"Can you make this?"

I already knew the answer.

"That's possible."

Since I'd decided to do it, I immediately looked for a domain too.

BabySweetDreams.com
