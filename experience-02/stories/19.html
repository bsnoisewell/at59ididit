19.
AI Eats TEXT.

After working with AI for a while, I started to understand some of its characteristics.

One of them:

AI is good at reading text.

Fast, too.

And it understands it pretty well.

As I built the website, my conversations with AI kept getting longer.

Then I ran into a problem.

Whenever I started a new conversation, I had to explain things again.

AI remembered some things, but there were limits.

What we'd done so far.

What we'd decided.

What we'd decided not to do.

I had to explain everything in detail again if I wanted to reduce mistakes.

When am I going to explain all of this?

So I simply copied the conversation into a text file and saved it.

Then, when I started a new conversation, I uploaded the file.

"Read this."

It read it quickly and understood.

Then we picked up where we'd left off.

Huh?

That's convenient.

From then on, it became a habit.

When things got long, I organized them into text.

When I handed work over to another AI, I gave it the text too.

Then I didn't have to explain everything again from the beginning.

If I'd had to spend hours explaining everything over and over, I probably couldn't have done it.

I just drop in a file.

It reads it quickly and gets back to work.

At some point, I started giving AI text before giving it work.

Like feeding it first.

AI eats TEXT.
