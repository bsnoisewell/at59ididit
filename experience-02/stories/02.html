2.
A Baby Whale Asleep in the Clouds. Drawing a Baby's Dream.

If babies were going to look at the site before falling asleep, I thought it needed characters.

An elephant.

A rabbit.

A puppy.

A whale.

A penguin.

All kinds of animals came to mind.

The problem was, I couldn't draw.

But I had AI.

"Make some animal characters that babies would like."

At first, I just let AI draw whatever it wanted.

We tried a few.

Hmm.

Not bad.

Then I took it a step further.

"Draw a baby whale sleeping in the clouds."

Wow.

That was pretty impressive.

A baby whale was asleep inside soft, billowing clouds.

There was a crescent moon,

and moonlight streamed through the clouds.

Ah.

Something about it made me feel good.

What if a baby looked at this before falling asleep,

then dreamed about playing with the whale in the clouds?

I decided to draw a baby's dream.
