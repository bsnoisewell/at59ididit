15.
Are Twin AIs Exactly the Same?

While building my second website, I started using several AIs at the same time.

I opened multiple ChatGPT conversations.

In one, I talked about design.

In another, audio.

In another, I checked code.

Then I suddenly got curious.

Hang on.

They're all ChatGPT.

So if I ask them exactly the same question, will they give me exactly the same answer?

I decided to test it.

"What instrument is best for making a baby lullaby?"

I copied the same question.

Pasted it into the first conversation.

Then pasted it into the second.

The answers came back.

At first, they were similar.

Felt/Soft Piano.

Music Box.

Harp.

Up to that point, they were almost the same.

But after that, they started to differ.

One mentioned Guitar and Celesta.

The other recommended instruments like Kalimba.

Similar.

But not exactly the same.

Huh?

They're both ChatGPT.

They do seem like twins.

But,

their personalities are a little different.
