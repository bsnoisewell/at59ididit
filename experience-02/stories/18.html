18.
I Opened the Door to My Storage Room for AI.

As I worked on the website with AI, the number of files kept growing.

Images.

Audio.

Excel files.

Program files.

Configuration files.

Several folders too.

Until then, whenever AI needed a file, I found it myself and showed it to AI.

Then a thought occurred to me.

"Hang on. Can't you just look at the files on my computer yourself?"

ChatGPT couldn't simply open folders on my computer and look for files.

But Codex was different.

If I opened the folder I wanted it to work in, it could look through the files inside,

read them,

and modify them if necessary.

Hmm.

This is completely different.

Until now, I'd been going into the storage room myself, finding what was inside, and bringing things out one by one.

Now it felt like I could just open the door.

"It's in there. You find it."

The way I worked had evolved.
