Asking ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude the Exact Same Question

Even different ChatGPT conversations give slightly different answers.

So what about other AIs?

I tried Gemini and Claude too.

Then I asked all of them exactly the same question.

"Compare ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude."

The answers were interesting.

"ChatGPT is an active co-writer, Claude is a literary editor, and Gemini is a smart researcher and organizer."

"ChatGPT is strong at general tasks and web browsing. For coding and long documents, Claude. For Google Workspace and multimodal tasks, Gemini. That's a common conclusion across many comparisons."

"ChatGPT is the all-rounder and final reviewer, Gemini excels at up-to-date research, and Claude is a great coding partner and careful writer."

Interesting.

Have we reached the point where we compare AIs and choose between them?

Then a thought occurred to me.

Until now, I'd built the site mostly with ChatGPT.

But after the site was finished,

what if I showed the exact same site to all three AIs and asked each of them for a final review?

ChatGPT.

Gemini.

Claude.

Looking at the same website,

would all three say the same thing?

Or,

would each one find something different that I had missed?
