The Clouds Turned Into Soap Bubbles.

The character illustrations were finished.

Now I started checking where each character was positioned and how their sleeping expressions looked.

I went through the characters one by one.

But something felt strange.

I went back to the first illustration.

The clouds looked soft and fluffy.

The next one looked fine too.

But as I kept going, they started changing little by little.

By the time I reached the last few, the clouds looked strange.

They looked like soap bubbles.

Or maybe little pieces of Styrofoam.

Huh?

When did this happen?

They had definitely been clouds at the beginning.

But as AI kept drawing, it seemed to have gradually changed them in its own way.

I'd never asked it to change the clouds.

In the end, I started drawing them all over again from the beginning.
